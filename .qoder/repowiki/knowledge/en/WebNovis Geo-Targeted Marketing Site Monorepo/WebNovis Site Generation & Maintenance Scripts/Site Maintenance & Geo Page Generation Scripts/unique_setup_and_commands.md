Geo generator is invoked as `node scripts/geo/main.js` with flags: `--dry-run`, `--validate-only`, `--type=all|agenzia|realizzazione|servizio|hubs`, `--out-dir=<path>`, `--report-dir=<path>`, `--city=<slug>,...`, `--service=<slug>,...`; outputs go to `PUBLISH_DIR` (default `.` or `build/public-artifact`) and reports to `REPORT_DIR`. Legacy `gen-geo-pages.js` runs standalone against `agenzia-web-rho.html` template. Scripts assume repository root layout with `data/`, `templates/`, `Img/`, and `config/` directories present.
