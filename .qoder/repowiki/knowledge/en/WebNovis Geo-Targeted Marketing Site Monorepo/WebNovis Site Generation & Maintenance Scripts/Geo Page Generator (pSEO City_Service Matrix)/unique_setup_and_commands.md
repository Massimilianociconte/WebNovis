Run via `node scripts/geo/main.js` with optional flags parsed from `process.argv`: `--type=all|agenzia|realizzazione|servizio|hubs`, `--dry-run`, `--validate-only`, `--out-dir=<path>` (defaults to `PUBLISH_DIR` env or `.`), `--report-dir=<path>` (defaults to `build/public-artifact` or `data`), `--city=slug1,slug2`, `--service=slug1,slug2`. Requires `data/cities.json`, `data/services.json`, and optionally `data/content-blocks/tier1-<city>-*.json` approved blocks; outputs land under `PUBLISH_DIR` and a `link-graph.json` report under `REPORT_DIR`.
