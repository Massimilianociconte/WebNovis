Requires `GEMINI_API_KEY_WRITER` (or `GEMINI_API_KEY`) and optionally `GROQ_API_KEY` in the project root `.env`. Run `node blog/auto-writer.js` to generate 10 articles, `node blog/auto-writer.js --dry-run` to preview without writing, `node blog/auto-writer.js --count 5` for a custom batch size, or `node blog/auto-writer.js --rebuild-index` to regenerate only `blog/index.html` and `sitemap.xml`. The script also expects `blog/topics-queue.json` to contain available topics with `used: false` entries.
